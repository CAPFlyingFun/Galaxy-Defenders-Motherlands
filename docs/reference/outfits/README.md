# Outfit reference images

Joshua's outfit style designs (rendered with ChatGPT, 2026-11-18), saved here at his say-so for designing the
heroines' outfits. They show the look to aim for, not finished art: colours and themes can change.

- `01-crop-jacket-red-black.png`: cropped red/black jacket with shoulder plates, bare belly, low belt, panelled
  leggings, heeled boots.
- `02-catsuit-gray-red.png`: a full gray suit with red panels and glowing lines, belly covered, shoulder plates,
  wrist cuffs, belt under the belly, heeled boots.
- `03-harness-thigh-boots.png`: a sleeveless crop top with shoulder plates, a harness down the sides to a low brief,
  forearm guards, thigh-high heeled boots.
- `04-four-looks-board.png`: four looks (shadow catsuit, anime space heroine, fantasy star knight, superhero
  vanguard), a catsuit at 0, 80, 140 and 200 % belly, and rear views.

- `look1-shadow-operative-closeup.jpg`: Look 1 up close (Joshua, 2026-11-19): the collar up her neck, the stripes, the belt.
- `ccbs-fabric-colours.jpg`: the Character Builder fabric pack's sixteen colours (the suit uses its athletic knit).
- `look2-anime-space-heroine-front.jpg` / `-back.jpg`: Look 2 (the Anime Space Heroine) up close, front and back
  (Joshua, 2026-11-19: "try and make it as close as possible").
